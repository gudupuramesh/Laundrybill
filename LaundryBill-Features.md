## 0) Positioning (one-liners)

- **Product:** Laundry Bill — POS & Manager for laundry, dry-clean & ironing businesses.
- **Tagline:** *Run your laundry business from your pocket.*
- **For:** single laundry shops **and** multi-outlet laundry chains.
- **Platforms:** iOS app · Android app · Web dashboard · customer-facing public pages · role apps (Staff, Driver/Agent, Plant).
- **Pricing:** Free to start · Pro for single shops · Business for chains.
- **Elevator pitch:** *Laundry Bill replaces the paper register with one simple app — bill customers in seconds, track every order from pickup to delivery, send receipts and updates on WhatsApp, let customers track their order online, manage staff and money, and scale from one shop to a full chain with drivers, a central plant, and online ordering.*

---

## 1) The Problem (why shops need this)

- Paper slips & registers get **lost, smudged, or mixed up** — orders fall through the cracks.
- **No order tracking** — staff and customers don't know what's pending, ready, or delivered.
- **Money leaks** — untracked dues, cash, and expenses; no clear daily profit.
- **No customer experience** — no receipts, no status updates, no online booking.
- **Staff blind spots** — no attendance, no role control, no accountability.
- **No data to grow** — can't see best customers, busy days, or true profit.
- **Chains can't scale** — no way to standardise outlets, route work to a plant, or manage drivers.

## 3) DETAILED FEATURE CATALOG

### A. Billing & Point of Sale
- **Create a bill in seconds** — add a customer, pick services & items, set quantities, and generate the bill instantly.
- **Your own services, items & pricing** — fully customisable catalog (wash, dry-clean, iron, etc.) with per-item rates and images.
- **Discounts & charges** — apply discounts and extra charges per order.
- **GST / Tax** — optional "Charge GST" toggle with configurable tax rate (e.g., 18%) and tax details on the bill.
- **Downloadable & printable receipts** — generate a clean receipt the customer can keep; **download/share it** or send to a thermal/Bluetooth printer.
- **One-click WhatsApp receipt** — send the bill straight to the customer on WhatsApp in a single tap.
- **Order numbers** — automatic, sequential order IDs.

### B. Order Management & Tracking
- **Full order lifecycle** — clear statuses from pickup → in-process → ready → delivered, with payment status (paid / due).
- **Order details screen** — items, amounts, customer, dates, notes — all in one place; edit anytime.
- **QR-code / barcode tags** — tag each bag and **scan to instantly open its order** — never mix up customers' clothes.
- **Garment / damage photos** — attach photos of item condition to protect against disputes *(Business)*.
- **Public order tracking link** — every order gets a link customers can open (no app needed) to see live status — fewer "is it ready?" calls.
- **Automatic status notifications** — customers and staff are notified as orders move (order created / updated).

### C. Customer Management (CRM)
- **Customer profiles** — name, phone, email, address.
- **Full order & payment history** per customer.
- **Outstanding dues tracking** — know exactly who owes what.
- **One-tap Call & WhatsApp** to any customer.
- **Add from phone contacts** — pull a customer in from your address book (read on-device only).

### D. Catalog — Services & Items
- **Service categories** (Wash & Fold, Dry Clean, Ironing, etc.).
- **Items with pricing & images** under each service.
- **Edit anytime** — change prices and offerings as your shop changes.

### E. Staff & HR
- **Staff roster** — add staff with role and salary details.
- **Role-based staff logins** — give limited logins for **Staff**, **Driver/Agent**, and **Plant** roles so each person sees only what they need.
- **Attendance** — mark daily attendance with one tap (Present / Absent / Half-day / Leave); auto-saves; monthly summary and days-worked per staff.
- **Payroll** — compute pay from attendance and pay type (monthly / per-day).

### F. Money, Reports & Insights
- **Dashboard "This Month"** — collections, outstanding, and order count at a glance.
- **Expenses tracking** — log and categorise shop expenses.
- **Finances / net profit** — income vs expenses, daily and monthly.
- **Reports & analytics** — collections, busy days, top customers, profit trends.

### G. Customer-Facing Experience
- **Public ordering page** — customers place orders online; **time-slot booking** with live slot availability *(Business)*.
- **Order tracking link** — self-service status tracking (Pro+).
- **WhatsApp updates** — receipts and "order ready" messages in one tap.

### H. Chain & Scale (Business)
- **Driver / Agent app** — manage pickups and deliveries on the road.
- **Plant processing app** — route garments to a central plant and track them through each processing stage.
- **Staff app** — counter logins for each outlet with controlled access.
- **Unlimited staff accounts** and role-based access.
- **Web dashboard access** — manage the operation from a desktop.
- **Damage photos**, **public ordering**, and the full Pro toolkit across outlets.

### I. Platform, Trust & Admin
- **Works in 9 languages** — English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati.
- **Cloud sync** — data is safe and in sync across devices and the web.
- **Sign-in options** — Email/Password, Google, and Apple Sign-In.
- **Push notifications** — order and account updates.
- **In-app Feedback** — owners send issues/suggestions from Settings; the team sees them in the admin.
- **In-app help videos** — tutorial videos on key screens.
- **Subscriptions & billing** — Free / Pro / Business plans, **free trial**, in-app purchase (Apple/Google), discount **coupons**, and grace-period handling.
- **Account deletion** — full in-app account deletion (privacy-compliant).

## 4) Use Case — The Single Laundry Shop (Pro)

**A day at the counter:**
1. Customer drops clothes → owner creates a bill in seconds and **shares the receipt on WhatsApp**.
2. Each bag gets a **QR tag**; staff **scan to find any order** later.
3. Customer gets an **order-tracking link** to check status themselves.
4. When ready, one tap sends an **"order ready" WhatsApp** message.
5. Owner marks **staff attendance**, logs **expenses**, and sees **today's collection & profit** — no calculator, no register.

**Benefits:** bill & deliver faster · never lose an order · collect every due · look professional to customers · know your true numbers.

---

## 5) Use Case — The Laundry Chain (Business)

**How an operation runs:**
1. Customers order via the **public ordering page** (with **time-slot booking**) or at any outlet.
2. **Drivers** pick up using the **Driver/Agent app**.
3. Garments are routed to the **central plant** and tracked through processing in the **Plant app**.
4. **Staff** at each outlet work with **role-based logins** (limited access).
5. **Damage photos** protect against disputes; customers track everything via the **tracking link**.
6. Owners manage **attendance, payroll, expenses and consolidated reports** from the **web dashboard**.

**Benefits:** standardise every outlet · centralise processing · control staff access by role · bring in orders from every channel · see the whole operation's collections, payroll and profit in one place.

## 10) Design Notes (so the deck stays on-brand)

- **Colors:** primary blue `#1B61E5` / deep blue `#0148A2`, navy `#13253D`; light backgrounds `#EEF4FF`; **green accent `#0FB57A`** for money/chain/success; alert red `#EF4444` only on the Problem slide.
- **Structure:** dark navy slides for Title / section breaks / closing; light slides for content ("sandwich").
- **Motif:** rounded cards with soft shadows; each feature gets an **icon in a colored rounded-square chip**.
- **Type:** a strong header font (e.g., Poppins / Georgia) + a clean body font (Calibri / Inter). Titles 32–44pt, body 14–16pt.
- **Visuals:** drop **real app screenshots** into slides 5–11 (dashboard, new order, QR scan, customer detail, attendance, finances, public tracking). Use phone mockups.
- **Tone:** practical and benefit-led; speak to a shop owner, not a developer.
- **Don't:** overcrowd slides, use tiny text, or put everything in one color — let blue dominate with a green accent.
